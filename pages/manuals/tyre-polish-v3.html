No paddle on hand — use a whisk + blender: a paddle is just a low-shear stirring tool for the "gentle/low speed" steps (EDTA dissolve, IPA, preservatives, dye, fragrance, final top-up). A hand whisk covers these fine — fold slowly, don't whip air in. "Medium speed" steps (XG sprinkle-in, emulsifier blend pour-in) use the blender on a low setting. "High/maximum speed" steps (Phase B's 5-minute mix, Phase D's critical 8-minute blend) need the blender at full speed — nothing substitutes for these, they're what actually builds the emulsion. If hand-whisking introduces foam, rest the batch a few extra minutes before the next addition to let bubbles settle.

04Silicone Emulsion — active % adjustment

This formula is now dosed on a 55% active Silicone Emulsion (272.7 g/L → 150 g active) — the strength actually being stocked. Real drums can still vary batch to batch per COA. Dose to a fixed active weight, not a fixed raw weight, so gloss and rain-resistance stay consistent.

Formula: Adjusted g = 150 ÷ (active% ÷ 100). Add the difference above 272.7 g to the Silicone Emulsion, and subtract that same amount from the Phase E RO Water top-up so total batch weight stays exact. For 2 L / 3 L / 5 L batches, scale the 150 g target and the table values by the batch size.

05Quality control & troubleshooting

Run every check on every batch, regardless of size. Fail on pH or stability = do not release.
ParameterAcceptable rangeIf it fails
pH5.8 – 6.2Above 6.2 → add citric acid dropwise. Below 5.8 → add 10% NaOH dropwise.
AppearanceSmooth, opaque white–pale cream gelLumps → XG not hydrated. Continue stirring 10 min.
Batch weightTarget ± 0.5%Top up with RO water and record the variance in the batch log.
ViscosityThick coat on spoon, flows slowly on tiltToo thin → XG under-hydrated. Rest 20 min, retest.
Oil layerNone after 30 min restOil layer → emulsification insufficient. Re-blend Phase D, 8 more minutes.
Dye uniformityUniform, no streaksStreaks → dye added before pH was confirmed. Mix 5 min more.
Gloss test (RTU)Shiny, even black, no streaking on sidewallStreaky → check dimethicone level and Phase D emulsification.
4-week stabilityNo separation, pH drift <0.3, no colour shiftSeparation → emulsifier ratio issue. Add lecithin (5 g/L), re-blend.

Before the first commercial run at any batch size: fill two extra bottles, age one at 40–45°C and one at 5°C for 4 weeks, then re-check pH, appearance, viscosity and gloss. Release only if both pass.
