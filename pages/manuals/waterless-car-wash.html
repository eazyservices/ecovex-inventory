03

Step-by-step process

Never reuse a Phase A vessel for Phase B without washing and drying it first — leftover anionic residue destabilizes the cationic amino silicone.

1
Charge Phase A water—1 min
Measure distilled water into the mixing vessel. Confirm distilled — never RO or tap.
2
Disperse HECWhisk, low–med5–7 minCheckpoint
In a separate cup, stir HEC into Propylene Glycol until a smooth, lump-free paste. Pour into the Phase A water while whisking continuously; keep whisking until completely clear — no fish-eyes or gel lumps.
Reject / do not proceed if: any fish-eyes, gel lumps, or cloudiness remain after 7 min.
3
Build Phase AWhisk, low–med~17 min total
Add one at a time, whisking 2–3 min between each: Propylene Glycol (remainder) → Glycerin → Polyquaternium-7 → GHPTC (pre-mixed in warm water) → Sodium Gluconate (dissolve fully) → C8/C10 APG → Sodium Benzoate (dissolve fully) → IPA 90%, added last, whisk 2 min. Keep the vessel covered between additions — IPA is flammable and evaporates quickly. Result: clear to slightly hazy, no undissolved particles.
4
Pre-blend Phase BBlend, high3 min
In a separate, clean, fully dried cup — never one that touched Phase A or any surfactant — combine PEG-40 HCO, Polysorbate 20, Dimethicone, and BHT solution. Blend on high until uniform, slightly hazy to clear.
5
Add silicone emulsionBlend, medium3–4 min
Slowly pour Amino Silicone Emulsion into the Step 4 pre-blend on medium speed, steadily over 3–4 min — don't dump it in. Keep the mixture cool to the touch; if it feels warm, pause and let it cool before continuing (unscheduled rest, judge by touch, not the clock).
6
Extended homogenisationBlend, high8–10 minCritical control point
Full/high speed, continuous, timed with a clock — this determines whether the emulsion holds at 1:200 dilution. Result: smooth, white to off-white, no grainy texture, no free oil/silicone sheen on the surface.
Reject / do not proceed if: grainy texture, any oil/silicone sheen, or the mixture is still warm to the touch. Re-blend 2–3 more minutes before moving on.
7
Combine Phase B into Phase AWhisk, low3–4 min pour + 5 min mixCheckpoint
Slowly pour the Phase B blend into the main Phase A vessel while whisking gently on low — never the stick blender here, it can over-shear and invert the emulsion. Pour over 3–4 min, then whisk 5 more minutes.
Reject / do not proceed if: rings on the vessel sides, visible separation, or a thin, watery look (sign of over-shearing).
8
Add Phase CWhisk, low–med~11 min
Add Coco Glucoside, whisk 3 min. Add CAPB, whisk 3 min. Then whisk 5 more minutes. Result: uniform milky white to light-blue liquid.
9
Add Phase D & correct pHWhisk, low~13 min + pH checkCheckpoint
Add Phenoxyethanol, whisk 3 min. Add Ethylhexylglycerin, whisk 5 min. Check pH. Add Citric Acid 50%, a few drops at a time, whisking and re-checking pH after each addition, until 5.5–6.0. Don't dump it all in at once — see the pH guide below.
Reject / do not proceed if: pH remains outside 5.5–6.0 after dropwise correction.
10
Add Phase E, top upWhisk, low5 min
Add Colorant first, whisk briefly. In a small dish, whisk Polysorbate 20 with Fragrance Oil until one clear, uniform liquid — do not add the fragrance neat. Stir the premix into the batch last. Top up with distilled water to exact volume in a graduated cylinder. Whisk 5 min to fully blend.
11
Final QC & bottle—Checkpoint
Check pH (5.5–6.0) and appearance (uniform colour, no rings, no separation). Bottle into clean, sealed containers, label with batch date. Gentle inversion test — invert once; should remix within seconds, no lumps or oily streaks.
Reject / do not proceed if: any ring, cloudy band, two-layer look, or slow/incomplete remix on inversion.
04

pH correction guide

Target for release: pH 5.5–6.0, checked at Step 9 and reconfirmed at Step 11.

Above 6.0Add D3 Citric Acid a few drops at a time (never the full amount at once). Whisk 1–2 min after each addition and re-check. Stop as soon as you're in range.
Below 5.5Too much citric acid was added, usually from dosing too fast. Do not add any alkali to correct it back up — this formula has no base-correction step. Instead dilute the error out with small increments of the E3 top-up water, re-checking pH between additions. If you've used the full E3 allowance and are still under 5.5, mark the batch off-spec — don't release it.
Electrolyte cautionNever increase Sodium Gluconate (A6) or Sodium Benzoate (A8) beyond spec to try to fix pH or anything else — both lower the cloud point of the nonionic surfactants and can push the batch toward instability under heat.
05

Post-production hold checks

Mandatory before release — not part of active blending, these confirm the batch stays stable over time.

24-hour lookClear glass, undisturbed, room temperature, 24 hrs. Fail if any layer, ring, or cloudy band top or bottom.
Warm spot testSealed sample near a window or on a fridge top, 1 week — simulates Hyderabad transport/storage heat. Fail on any visible separation.
2-week shelf sampleOne untouched bottle, room temp, checked Day 3 / 7 / 14. Fail at any checkpoint = batch is off-spec, do not release.

A batch may not be labelled, boxed, or released until it has cleared the 24-hour look at minimum. Hold for the full warm-spot result before large production runs.

06

Visual rejection criteria

CreamingA distinct lighter/whitish or clear layer pooling at the top near the neck — the amino silicone/dimethicone phase creaming out. Automatic reject.
Ring / bandAny ring or band visible at the liquid's surface line, even faint.
Oily streaksStreaks left on the glass after a gentle inversion, or slow/incomplete remixing.
Grainy textureGritty feel when the bottle is tilted.
Oily layer at topUnder-blended Step 6, or B1/B3 not weighed accurately (emulsifier-to-oil ratio off).
Haze worsens with warmthElectrolyte issue — A6/A8 too high, lowering cloud point. Do not increase these to fix anything else.
Separation right after Step 7Cross-contamination between Phase A/B vessels, or stick blender used instead of whisk in Step 7.
pH drifted low, then separatesOver-acidified batch destabilising the cationic amino silicone — see the pH guide, do not add alkali.

If any rejection criterion is present at any checkpoint — reject the batch. Do not re-shake or re-sell; re-blend fresh or discard.

07

Batch release sign-off

  • Step 6 full high-speed homogenisation completed and timed
  • No cross-contamination between Phase A / Phase B vessels
  • A9 (IPA 90%) added in Phase A build, quantity confirmed
  • Distilled water confirmed for A1 and E3 — no RO substitution used
  • pH confirmed 5.5–6.0 at Step 9 and reconfirmed at Step 11
  • 24-hour look passed — no layer, ring, or cloudy band
  • Warm spot test passed, or in progress and batch held pending result
  • Inversion test passed — remixes within seconds, no streaks
  • Batch date and ID labelled on all bottles
