06

Troubleshooting

Won't thicken past a thin liquid
Confirm SLES/CAPB are at full quantity and that the chelator (EDTA or DIY citrate) fully dissolved with no clumps — the salt curve only structures with enough surfactant and no undissolved solids.
Thickened, then suddenly went thin and cloudy
Past the salt-curve peak — can't be fixed by adding more salt. Start a fresh test batch and titrate slower, checking every 20–25 ml instead of larger jumps.
Thicker cold, thinner warm
Normal, mildly temperature-sensitive. Shake or warm slightly before pouring — not separation.
Grainy specks after adding brine
Salt wasn't fully dissolved before adding. Always pre-dissolve as a 10% brine, never add dry crystals directly.
Pours fine but feels stringy
Stirring was too vigorous (whisking/blending) during thickening. Redo with slow, gentle paddle stirring only.
Batch fizzed after adding chelator
Only relevant on the DIY citrate route — the CO₂ reaction wasn't finished before adding to the batch. Let it settle 5–10 min fully next time.
07

Storage, Cleaning & Safety

  • Shelf life: 12–18 months in a dark, wide-spout PET or glass bottle, stored below 25°C, out of direct sun.
  • If stiffer than usual after cold storage, shake or stir gently before use — normal, not a failure.
  • Always dispense with a dry pump or spoon; never pour used wash water back into the bottle.
  • Rinse jars and utensils immediately after use — SLES/CAPB dry into a sticky film once set.
  • For mineral scale on glass, soak 5–10 min in warm water with a splash of white vinegar, then scrub.
  • Final rinse with distilled/RO water, especially for anything used near pH testing.
  • Keep this equipment dedicated to this formula line — never let it cross over with anything that touched bleach.
Safety: Isopropyl alcohol is flammable — mix in a ventilated area, away from open flames. Do not mix with bleach under any circumstances. Do not ingest; rinse thoroughly on skin/eye contact. Formulated for synthetic microfiber only — not for cotton, wool, or silk. Keep out of reach of children and pets.
